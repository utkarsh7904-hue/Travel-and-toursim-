## Libraries and map data

- Leaflet 1.9.4 files are included in `frontend/public/vendor/`. The full BSD-2-Clause licence is in `licenses/leaflet-BSD-2-Clause.txt`.
- The CSS reset is adapted from Tailwind CSS preflight. Its MIT licence is in `licenses/tailwindcss-MIT.txt`.
- npm-installed dependencies include their own licence notices; they are fetched by `npm install` and are not bundled in this source ZIP.
- Map data: © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). Keep visible attribution. Standard tiles are loaded on demand; the app does not prefetch or bulk-download them. Consider an appropriate tile hosting service before a high-traffic public rollout.
- DM Sans, Manrope and Noto Sans Kannada are loaded from Google Fonts. Georgia is a system serif font.

## Destination references

Each destination record contains its official visitor-information source and, where relevant, a UNESCO record. Text was independently summarised for the project. Heritage information is dated; operational status is not tracked live.
